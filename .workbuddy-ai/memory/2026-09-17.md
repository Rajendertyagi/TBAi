## Pre-existing bug found: TerminalBlock dropped its own `status` prop

`terminal-block.tsx` declares and documents `status?: "success" | "error"` and
branches on it in the body, but omits it from the destructuring list — so the body
read the global `window.status`. Effect: `status === "error"` was never true, a
failed command rendered a green `exit 0` badge, and `RunCommandTerminalUI`'s own
`status="error"` was silently dropped. One-line fix; the file is otherwise vendored
verbatim and marked "do not edit", so the deviation is recorded in its header.
`terminal-block.test.ts` locks it in (mutation-verified: removing the
destructuring fails 3 of 4).
